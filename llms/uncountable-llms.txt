# Uncountable

> Uncountable is a unified laboratory informatics platform for R&D organizations. It replaces disconnected LIMS, ELN, and data tools with a single cloud-based system that centralizes experimental data, accelerates formulation, and enables AI-driven discovery.

## Products

- [Unified R&D Platform](https://uncountable.com/cloud-platform): End-to-end platform connecting R&D, QC, and PLM data for faster innovation.
- [LIMS](https://uncountable.com/products/integrated-laboratory-information-management-system-lims): Integrated laboratory information management system for enterprise R&D.
- [ELN](https://uncountable.com/products/integrated-electronic-laboratory-notebook-eln): Electronic laboratory notebook with structured data capture and visualization.
- [Quality Management System](https://uncountable.com/products/quality-management-system): QMS for R&D and manufacturing compliance.
- [Product Lifecycle Management](https://uncountable.com/products/product-lifecycle-management-for-enterprises): PLM for enterprise formulation and product development.
- [QC LIMS](https://uncountable.com/products/integrated-qc-lims-for-enterprises): Integrated quality control LIMS for enterprise labs.
- [Visualizations & Reporting](https://uncountable.com/products/integrated-visualizations-reporting): Advanced data visualization and reporting for R&D insights.
